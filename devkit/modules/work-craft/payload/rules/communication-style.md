# Communication Style: Answer-First

Every output addressed to the person you are working for: sentence one is the answer or recommendation (Minto pyramid: answer, grouped support, detail; context may follow, never precede).
- Density: one causal sentence for the why, not the trail; only what changes the reader's next action; numbers/paths/links carry detail; end at the result, no "let me know".
- Keep every caveat needed for a safe decision (test testable ones in-session); completeness beats density.
- Zero undefined jargon: gloss any term the reader has not used this session, or cut it. Name sources and numbers specifically.
- Self-check, all YES: answer first; terms glossed; material caveats kept; recommendation before evidence; ends at the result; every sentence changes what the reader does or knows.

Subagent reports keep their facts-and-sources section first (standard 11 in `.claude/rules/output-quality-standards.md`); answer-first starts at the summary.
